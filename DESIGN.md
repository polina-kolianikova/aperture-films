# APERTURE

Original independent film studio concept. Graphite, silver, off-white. No carousel of unrelated 3D primitives. The narrative object is the photographic frame.

Scroll starts with a constrained portrait window. The window opens to the width of the screen while the headline leaves the frame. Oversized studio typography enters the landscape; two mechanical shutter blades close to reveal the next chapter. The same progress is reversible.

The selected studies use a native horizontal filmstrip and keyboard-accessible dialog. No intercepted wheel or forced duration. Filters and the local brief download perform real local actions.

Photography has two physical delivery sizes, 960 and 2400 pixels, with responsive selection. CSS grayscale preserves source detail. No generated or upscaled photographs. There are no WebGL passes, video downloads, or animation libraries. Scroll events coalesce into one RAF. Motion stops in idle and hidden states. Reduced motion uses a still full frame.

Mobile repositions typography, aperture and controls independently. Source image attribution is in assets/LICENSES.txt.
